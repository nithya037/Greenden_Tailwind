About us

From balcony gardens to corporate landscapes – we design, maintain, and care for green spaces that thrive all year round.

Garden Maintenance

Lawn care, pruning & upkeep to maintain a neat and vibrant look. Soil treatment - composting, pH balancing, and amendment for better growth.

Vertical Gardens Walls

Innovative vertical gardening solutions for homes, corporates, and public spaces. Modular green wall systems designed for easy installation.

Corporate Plant Rentals
